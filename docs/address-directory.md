### United States

New Rochelle is the main American town: Lawton, Brook, Albemarle, Potter, Winyah, North Avenue, Coligni, Mechanic, Horton, Oak, and the Wykagyl and Premium Point households. Connecticut holds Bridgeport (Hamilton Street and Hallett Street) and Hartford (Goodman Place and 397 Woodland Street). Florida holds Daytona Beach, Ormond Beach, Hollywood, and Atlantis. Minnesota, Nevada, New Jersey, Ohio, and West Virginia also appear. Vermont is named only by an unverified death index for Jeffery, so it is not a directory entry. It is in the open questions.

### Ireland

Conahy, County Kilkenny, is the baptism place of Patrick Hennessy on 12 April 1874. His 1905 declaration gives the county. No ship is known for his arrival at New York on 11 January 1894.

### Italy

San Bartolomeo in Galdo, in the province of Benevento, is the comune. The town hall (casa comunale) is where the civil marriages were recorded. The port of Naples is the departure port named on the Hindoustan, Alsatia, and Sant'Anna lists. The Oregon index says that ship came from Genoa and Naples, and this directory does not choose which of those two ports the family boarded.
